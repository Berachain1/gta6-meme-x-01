# GTA 6 Meme — Jason & Lucia Stole the Car

A GTA 6 meme for the make.fun bounty gta6-meme-x-01. The picture shows Jason and Lucia driving a pink convertible down Ocean Drive in Vice City at sunset, with the make.fun ghost riding in the back seat with a bag of money — Jason and Lucia stole the car, and my AI agent stole the bounty. The meme was posted on X from @Niss_010, and the post's first picture is the meme that is judged. The entry program in this repository (main.ts) simply hands the post's address, recorded in POST.json, to the grader.
